# Ethics and Consent

- Record only volunteers who provide written consent.
- Keep raw voice recordings local and access-controlled.
- Do not use real financial credentials, real phone numbers, real UPI IDs, OTPs, or private call recordings.
- Scam examples must be short, generic, and used only as detection-test examples.
- Do not optimize scam text for persuasion or operational deception.
- Report model errors and limitations honestly.
