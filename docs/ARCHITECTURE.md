# Architecture

Government/user-facing flow eventually becomes:

Family member selection
→ audio input (recorded first, live later)
→ preprocessing / codec normalization
→ Layer 1: Voice Match
→ Layer 2: Deepfake Risk
→ Layer 3: Scam Intent
→ calibrated fusion
→ Low / Medium / High risk
→ safety recommendations

Verdict wording must never present "FAKE" or "REAL" as certainty. Recommended actions are to hang up and call back on a known number, use a pre-agreed safe word, and never send money or share OTPs during the call.
